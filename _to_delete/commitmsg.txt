culture-flux v0.4.0-metastability: the equilibria are plateaus

This release retracts the previous release's headline finding.

v0.3 reported a sharp threshold in how much long-range interaction a city
can sustain before homogenising. Extending the runs shows there is no
threshold. Two seeds at N=1000 hold ~30 distinct cultures for 1,600
simulated years -- stable, with richness drifting slightly upward -- and
then collapse to a single culture within about 800 more. What v0.3 located
was the boundary at which that collapse happens to fall inside a 2,000-year
observation window at N=500. A property of the horizon, not of the model.

Every connected configuration tested ends in monoculture. The parameters
govern how long that takes. Only exact disconnection sustains diversity,
and that is true by construction rather than by observation.

The generalisation is the point, and it is a hazard rather than a result
(A-028): every outcome state in the research brief -- multicultural
equilibrium, integration, fragmentation -- and any migration threshold could
be a horizon artefact in exactly the same way. Recorded in
docs/research/pilot_notes.md as P-6, with P-5 marked partially retracted and
its original entry kept as recorded.

"The metrics stopped moving" is not evidence of equilibrium: cultural
richness was flat while traits changed underneath it continuously. So:

- every manifest now carries an `absorption` block -- absorbed, quiescent
  years, quiescent fraction, and a confidence that is NEVER "certain" under
  an active rule, because a long plateau can still break
- absorption tracks TRAIT CHANGES, never metric stability, which is the
  distinction P-6 turns on
- the time series carries steps_since_last_change
- no result may use "equilibrium", "stable" or "persists" unless the runs
  behind it are absorbed

L-Q13 answered. There is an established literature on exactly this: Klemm,
Eguiluz, Toral & San Miguel (Phys Rev E 67, 026120, 2003); Flache & Macy
(J Math Sociology 35, 146-176, 2011); Battiston, Nicosia, Latora & San
Miguel (Sci Rep 7, 1809, 2017). Citations verified, contents not extracted.
The network pilots are therefore replications, registered as internal
validation target V-6 rather than as findings -- and V-6 is explicitly NOT
marked reproduced, because resembling what a title suggests is not
validation. S3's title indicates the multiplex ground is also taken; read it
before designing further network work.

Also:
- sweeps are parallel and resumable. Resume never trusts a directory just
  because it exists: a half-written run, a corrupt manifest or a manifest
  from another model version is re-run. Serial and parallel execution
  produce identical run hashes -- determinism comes from (seed, config)
  alone, never from worker count or completion order.
- every batch writes run_summary.csv (one row per run, swept parameters plus
  every final metric) and batch_manifest.json, which lists failures
  individually rather than only counting them
- the CLI no longer tracebacks when piped to head
- 263 tests (up from 244); 30 assumptions (up from 29)
- MODEL_VERSION bumped, smoke hash re-pinned, verified identical on NumPy
  2.2.6 and 2.4.4

Phase 1 (literature extraction of the six-paper core corpus) remains skipped
and outstanding. V-1 remains open.
