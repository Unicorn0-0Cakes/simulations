culture-flux v0.5.0-two-rules: the mechanism matters more than the migration

Adds a second transmission rule from a different family, cultural drift, and
a third RNG tier -- and with them, the first answer to the project's own
central question.

The two rules disagree about the DIRECTION of the migration effect. Same
city, same arrivals, same seeds, M=0.30, compositional baseline 0.850:
homophilous copying gives retention 0.73 (residents pulled towards the
migrants), conformist transmission gives 0.9999 (migrants almost entirely
assimilated). A single-rule study would have been confidently wrong in
whichever direction its author happened to pick. A-016 required two rules
before any result; this is what that requirement was for.

And RQ2 returns a null. Holding total migration constant, splitting the
incoming population into ten cultures instead of one made no detectable
difference to retention under either rule (+0.03 +/- 0.06 and -0.00). That
is H0-1, and it contradicts H1, H2 and H3 alike. Four seeds at one parameter
point -- a pilot pointing at a design, not a result -- but the mechanism
moved the outcome far more than the source diversity did. If that survives a
proper design, the research questions are aimed at the wrong variable.

Cultural drift (the A-018 sensitivity) shows the monoculture result of every
earlier pilot was conditional on drift_rate=0, a default inherited from the
rule family rather than chosen. Above ~1e-3 the city stops collapsing at
all. But a control (P-8) shows the homophily arm at that rate is
indistinguishable from drift with no interaction whatsoever -- it was
measuring the noise floor. The first attempt at the three-arm comparison ran
there and is discarded rather than reported. Every result must now state
whether it is transmission- or drift-dominated.

RNG streams are now seeded in three nested tiers -- baseline (the city),
scenario (the arrivals), condition (network and dynamics) -- so a comparison
across transmission rules faces byte-identical arrivals as well as a
byte-identical city. Without it the three arms were not a controlled
contrast, which a test written for that purpose caught. RNG_LAYOUT_VERSION
bumped accordingly.

Also:
- conformity is one parameter spanning anti-conformity (<1), neutral
  unbiased transmission (=1) and conformity (>1), so H2's claim about a
  conformity asymmetry is directly testable (A-030). The default of 3.0
  saturates the outcome and should be swept, not treated as a reference.
- configs/sweeps/rq2_three_arm.json is RQ2 as a design
- 297 tests (up from 263); 31 assumptions (up from 30)
- smoke hash re-pinned to 87c45b24b5e46fd3f6fc4603ab2ea713, verified
  identical on NumPy 2.2.6 and 2.4.4

Phase 1 (literature extraction) remains skipped. V-1 open. V-7 unresolved and
now the priority read: S5's title points the opposite way from pilot P-7.
