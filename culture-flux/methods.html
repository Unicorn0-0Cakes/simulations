01 The source

There isn't one, and that is the most important thing on this page

The transmission mechanisms in this model were specified in its own source code. They were not extracted from any published paper. A reading corpus was assembled at the start of the project and the extraction step was deliberately skipped, at the operator's direction, in order to build the mechanism sooner.

This is registered in the project's assumption registry as A-016 and described there as the least-supported decision in the codebase. It is why this page carries no basis badge: that badge names a documented source, and naming one here would be false.

The corpus that was not read

Held in docs/literature/literature_matrix.md with citations verified — venue, year and DOI checked against publisher pages — and every column describing what the papers actually contain marked NOT YET EXTRACTED. Nothing in this model derives from any of them.

  • Mesoudi, A. (2018). Migration, acculturation, and the maintenance of between-group cultural variation. PLOS ONE 13(10), e0205573.
  • Paolillo, R. & Jager, W. (2020). Simulating acculturation dynamics between migrants and locals in relation to network formation. Social Science Computer Review 38(4), 365–386.
  • Axelrod, R. (1997). The dissemination of culture. Journal of Conflict Resolution 41(2), 203–226.
  • Erten, E. Y., van den Berg, P. & Weissing, F. J. (2018). Acculturation orientations affect the evolution of a multicultural society. Nature Communications 9, 58.

Prior art found afterwards

Two of this instrument's own findings turned out to sit inside an existing literature, discovered only when they were checked before write-up. Both are therefore treated as replications rather than results, and neither has been compared quantitatively against the published work, because that work has not been read either.

  • Klemm, K., Eguíluz, V. M., Toral, R. & San Miguel, M. (2003). Nonequilibrium transitions in complex networks. Phys. Rev. E 67, 026120.
  • Klemm, K., Eguíluz, V. M., Toral, R. & San Miguel, M. (2003). Global culture: a noise-induced transition in finite systems. Phys. Rev. E 67, 045101(R).
  • Flache, A. & Macy, M. W. (2011). Small worlds and cultural polarization. Journal of Mathematical Sociology 35(1–3), 146–176.
  • Battiston, F., Nicosia, V., Latora, V. & San Miguel, M. (2017). Layered social influence promotes multiculturality in the Axelrod model. Scientific Reports 7, 1809.

The second of these is the priority read: its title states that noise drives a system towards a global culture, while this model observed drift sustaining diversity. Either the title compresses a non-monotone result, the noise enters differently, or this implementation is wrong. That is unresolved.

02 Rules and equations

Culture

An agent's culture is a vector of F categorical features, each holding one of Q traits: C = (c₁ … c_F). Cultural distance is the normalised Hamming distance — the fraction of features on which two agents differ, which is a true metric on this space and is verified as one in the test suite. Provenance (source_id) is a separate, immutable array; no mechanism may branch on it.

Migration

Migration share M is defined on the final population, so the addition and replacement modes end at the same composition and a contrast between them isolates whether residents left. Arrivals are allocated across source cultures by largest remainder — deterministic, conserving the total exactly, error bounded by one agent per source.

A source culture at target distance d differs from the resident profile on round(d·F) features. Only multiples of 1/F are achievable, so the realised distance matrix is measured and stored with each run; analysis uses the realised values, never the requested ones.

Transmission — the null

Nobody influences anybody. Retention is exactly 1 − M·D̄. This arm is permanent, not a placeholder: it is the control against which the others are read.

Transmission — homophilous copying

Per event: draw a focal agent; draw a partner through the multiplex network; compute cultural overlap ω = 1 − distance; with probability ω · attention(partner) the focal agent copies one feature on which they differ. Overlap of zero blocks influence entirely, so cultural distance is self-reinforcing and frozen configurations are reachable. Copying creates no new traits.

Transmission — conformist

Per event: draw a focal agent; sample n models from its network neighbourhood; choose one feature; adopt trait t with probability proportional to f_t^α, where f_t is that trait's frequency among the models. The exponent α spans anti-conformity (α<1), neutral unbiased transmission (α=1) and conformity (α>1) continuously. There is no similarity gate.

Cultural drift

Per agent per step, with probability δ, one feature changes to a different random trait. Without it, the set of traits present can only shrink and monoculture is the sole absorbing state; with it, there is no absorbing state at all. Drifting agents are drawn without replacement and the replacement trait always differs, so δ is the rate at which culture changes rather than at which a change is attempted.

The city

Household, neighbourhood and workplace layers assign each agent to one disjoint group; a friendship layer gives each agent a fixed number of directed attention ties that may rewire towards culturally similar agents; a citywide layer connects everyone. Each interaction event picks a layer in proportion to its weight, then a neighbour within it.

Measurement

Diversity indices are Hill numbers, so richness, exponentiated Shannon entropy and inverse Simpson are one family and directly comparable. Segregation is Theil's multi-group information index over residential units — chosen over the index of dissimilarity because dissimilarity is defined for two groups and cultural profiles are many, and collapsing them would require choosing which division matters.

04 What is simplified

No demography

Nobody is born, ages, dies or leaves. Migration generation only ever takes the values 0 and 1. Acculturation is usually studied across generations, and this model cannot see that timescale at all.

Cultural features are interchangeable

Every feature has equal weight, equal transmission rate and no resistance. Real cultures have cores and peripheries, and a model without them cannot represent partial retention — someone keeping what matters and shedding what does not — which is one of the outcomes the project was built to look for.

Influence is uniform across people

In these runs, everyone is equally likely to be copied, so group influence equals group population share. A model that reads influence from network position exists and was not used here. Any percentage threshold found under these runs is a statement about a world where influence is uniform.

Group membership is single and fixed

One household, one neighbourhood, one workplace, never changing. Arrivals are placed at random — real migration is strongly chain-structured, and random placement makes enclaves harder to obtain, which is the conservative error.

One undifferentiated noise term

Copying error, individual innovation and exogenous change are one parameter. They would scale differently with interaction rate, and a model separating them could answer differently.

No economics, politics, institutions, media or law

Nothing constrains anybody except who they meet. Discrimination, legal status and residential steering are all real mechanisms and none is present — and because no mechanism may branch on origin, adding them would require an explicit treatment layer rather than a quiet coupling.

05 What this cannot establish

Nothing about any real population

No output has ever been compared against data from any real city, and no parameter is fitted to anything. Every statement this instrument supports takes the form "conditional on these assumptions, the model does X". It cannot say what migration does to a place.

It cannot say which transmission mechanism is right. The two implemented rules disagree about the direction of the migration effect. The model has no way to adjudicate between them; that is an empirical question about people, and it was skipped.

It cannot report an equilibrium from a finite run. Two runs held a stable-looking cultural configuration for 1,600 simulated years before collapsing. Absorption is now recorded per run, and confidence is never reported as certain while a transmission rule is active.

It cannot estimate the probability of an outcome. The fragmentation hypothesis concerns how often an incoming culture becomes dominant. That happened zero times in 240 runs. Estimating a rate needs hundreds of runs per condition.

It cannot yet distinguish a null result from an underpowered one. The central finding — that the number of incoming cultures does not matter — rests on twenty seeds at one point in a six-dimensional parameter space.

It cannot claim novelty. Its two network findings were located inside an existing literature after the fact.

06 Validation status

Three kinds of validation are kept separate here, because conflating them is the usual way simulation work overclaims.

Verification — does the code implement the stated rules? Substantially complete

297 invariant tests, all passing under warnings-as-errors. They test scientifically meaningful properties rather than that functions return:

  • Cultural distance satisfies the metric axioms — identity, symmetry, boundedness, triangle inequality — and treats categorical traits as unordered.
  • Diversity indices match their closed forms: fractionalisation is exactly 1−1/k for k even categories, Hill numbers recover richness, exp-Shannon and inverse Simpson, and are non-increasing in order.
  • Migration accounting conserves totals exactly at every size tested; population never goes negative, including at 95% replacement.
  • Retention under the null equals 1 − M·D to machine precision — the compositional arithmetic, verified analytically rather than asserted.
  • Copying introduces no trait nobody held; zero overlap blocks influence exactly; a homogeneous population is absorbing.
  • Identical seed and configuration reproduce identical results, verified across Python 3.10/NumPy 2.2.6 and Python 3.11/NumPy 2.4.4, and pinned as a regression tripwire.
  • Serial and parallel sweep execution produce identical run hashes: determinism comes from seed and configuration alone, never from worker count or completion order.
  • No metric name encodes an outcome category or a threshold — enforced by a test, so the instrument cannot prejudge its own question.

Not verified: the parquet writer, behaviour above 20,000 agents, a second CPU architecture.

Internal validation — does it behave like its predecessors? Open

Three targets are registered and none is closed. Two concern behaviour that qualitatively resembles what the titles of published papers describe, which is not validation — resembling what a title suggests is exactly the failure the project's extraction protocol exists to prevent. Closing them requires reading the papers.

Empirical validation — does it correspond to reality? Not started

Not started, and not appropriate to start. The preconditions are recorded: a validated mechanism, the required sensitivity analyses run, a target observable named in advance, and a statement of what the model would have to produce to be wrong. There is also a deeper obstacle — no real dataset measures cultural-profile distributions over synthetic features, so any comparison needs a much coarser observable, and choosing one is itself unscheduled research.

Why the badge reads "Uncalibrated prototype"

That badge claims exactly two things: the model is internally consistent, and nothing is fitted to data. Both hold, and the list above is the work behind the first. It claims nothing about the mechanism being correct, which is just as well, because that is unknown.

07 Version history

0.5.0-two-rules — Aug 2026. Conformist transmission added as a second mechanism family; cultural drift; random streams re-tiered so that a comparison across mechanisms faces byte-identical arrivals as well as a byte-identical city. First answer to the central question, and it is a null. The two mechanisms disagree about the direction of the migration effect.

0.4.0-metastability — Aug 2026. Retracted the previous release's threshold: extended runs showed a 1,600-year plateau that then collapsed, so what had been located was an observation horizon. Absorption tracking added, by counting trait changes rather than watching whether summary statistics stopped moving.

0.3.0-structure — Aug 2026. Household, neighbourhood, workplace and attention-tie layers; segregation and modularity metrics; network-degree influence. Found that structure did not rescue cultural diversity — connectivity does, and overlapping local groups are one connected component.

0.2.0-homophily — Aug 2026. First transmission rule, chosen without the literature and registered as such.

0.1.0-skeleton — Aug 2026. Scientific skeleton: registries, determinism, culture representation, metrics, experiment runner. No dynamics — the null model only, as the permanent control arm.
